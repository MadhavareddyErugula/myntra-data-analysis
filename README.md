# Myntra Product Data Analysis Using Excel

An Excel analysis of Myntra product pricing, discounts, ratings, and brand and category comparisons.

## Dataset Overview
- 11,438 unique products
- 1,458 brands
- 245 categories
- Approximately 1.40 million ratings
- Average product rating: 4.16 out of 5

## Tools and Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Formulas for discount percentages and derived metrics
- PivotTables
- Charts and dashboards

## Dashboard Features
- Average marked and discounted price comparisons
- Average ratings by brand
- Brand and category comparisons by rating volume
- Engagement value comparisons

## Selected Findings
- Roadster has 80,847 ratings.
- Perfume and Body Mist has 125,303 ratings.
- MCaffeine has an average rating of 4.61, the highest among the five brands displayed.

## Metric Definitions
- **Rating volume:** Sum of product rating counts. This does not represent unique customers or units sold.
- **Average rating:** Unweighted average of product ratings.
- **Engagement value:** Discounted price multiplied by rating count, then summed. This is a derived comparison metric, not actual sales revenue.

## How to Explore
Download `Excel_dashboards.xlsx` and open it in Microsoft Excel. Start with the `LinkedIn_Dashboard` sheet, then explore the supporting data, PivotTables, and dashboards.
